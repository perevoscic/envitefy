# Envitefy review handoff

Selected arrangement, September 11, 2026: the user reviews the finished videos. Codex handles preparation, technical checks, independent AI critique, repair and recordkeeping before that handoff. No editor needs to be hired.

## Independent AI review assignment

During a video task, give a separate review subagent the actual rough cut or final file, the audience and objective, intended formats, product truth and brand references. Request an independent first-watch interpretation, followed by time-ranged findings with problem, viewer impact, severity and practical correction. Require the exact media inspection method, observed coverage and pending checks.

Inspect motion, performance, pacing, audio, spelling/pronunciation, captions, phone-size readability, actual product action/result and the ending. Review every requested final format. Do not infer full motion or listening from screenshots, a transcript or source code. The producer makes repairs; the independent reviewer verifies the changed export.

## What the user receives

- Playable final preview and direct link for each requested format, with clear version and duration.
- One sentence explaining the intended audience/message.
- A concise note only for any remaining limitation or a minor choice that needs their judgment.
- A simple invitation to approve or describe changes; the user need not supply timestamps or complete a checklist.

Keep internal review records in the campaign. The user may provide a broad reaction or describe an objection naturally; Codex translates it into actionable edits. Do not require user approval at script, shot-selection or rough-cut stages unless requested.

## Recording the decision

Keep the video ready for review until the user explicitly approves the presented version. Record their actual response and date against that MP4's SHA-256 in userApproval. Preserve the distinction between approval of one file and all formats. Requested changes lead to repairs, fresh internal checks and a new version for review. No approval is inherited by a changed export.

The detailed [quality workflow](quality.md) defines the release gate and evidence fields. Only the user can accept a remaining minor defect; blockers and major issues must be repaired. User approval does not authorize social publishing by itself.
