# Birthday expansion — September 7, 2026

Added 26 birthday templates, bringing the birthday catalog to 120.

Quick research informed the selection; these are editorial choices, not a ranked survey of children's preferences. We favored activities children can take part in, imaginative worlds, and practical party formats parents can organize.

Sources consulted:
- [Meri Meri — parties by age](https://merimeri.com/pages/party-by-age): age-based interests and animal, transport, and imaginative themes.
- [Dreamscape — children's birthday themes in 2026](https://dreamscape.net.au/blog/i/childrens-birthday-party-themes-2026): child-led interests, manageable celebrations, and hands-on activities.
- [PartySnap — kids' party themes](https://www.partysnap.co.uk/blog/11-most-popular-kids-party-themes-right-now-2026): slime, bracelets, pottery, and food crafts.

The additions span pottery, friendship beads, slime, magic school, roller skating, pixel building, sleepovers, pop performance, basketball, skateboarding, obstacle courses, detective games, dragons, axolotls, capybaras, bunnies, ponies, butterflies, jellyfish, pizza, sushi, lemonade markets, cherry picnics, storybooks, board games, and field days.

Each has dedicated artwork, an explicit hero composition, a distinct sample celebrant and venue, and practical host notes. Artwork was generated with the built-in image-generation tool and optimized to WebP in `public/templates/birthdays/redesign/next26/`. Exact prompts and original generated file paths are recorded in `generated-manifest.json`; `art-contact-sheet.png` shows all 26 assets.
