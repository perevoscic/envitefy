# Event Page artwork diversity review

The October 22, 2026 revision replaces repetitive new artwork across the 18 replacement collections. Existing Holidays & seasonal artwork remains available. A successful file decode or correct sports equipment does not establish visual diversity.

For each six-design subtype, inspect an unlabeled contact sheet first. Expect at least three distinct viewpoints, three shot scales, three focal placements, two lighting treatments and four setting or action ideas. Include photographic and illustrated work with intentional page palettes and typography. Allow at most two close object still lifes and one warm tabletop scene in the six. Changes to props, tint or background blur alone do not count as a new composition.

For each complete 60-design collection, inspect all ten subtype sheets together. Expect at least four visual media and at least twelve nonphotographic designs. Repeated framing across adjacent subtype sheets must be reworked even when each individual sheet meets the numerical checks. Artwork must remain recognizably specific to its event, sport or named tradition at thumbnail size.

Record two independent decisions for every installed asset:

- **Technical and semantic review:** source provenance, output hash, portrait dimensions, complete decode, appropriate anatomy and equipment, legible composition, no invented lettering or unintended religious imagery.
- **Diversity review:** medium, viewpoint, scale, focal placement, lighting, setting and narrative; retain or rework, with the compared cohort and a concrete reason.

Keep original source files and prior review evidence when an asset is replaced. A prior technical approval remains historical evidence; it does not approve that image under the revised diversity requirement. Publish a replacement collection only after all 60 installed files pass both reviews and gallery, editor, saved reload and guest rendering retain the intended artwork and authored page design.
