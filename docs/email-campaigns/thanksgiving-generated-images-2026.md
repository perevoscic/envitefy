Southern Envitefy Thanksgiving email. All seven images are new campaign art,
not reused catalog or marketing photography.

Shared direction: photorealistic candid editorial photography, warm natural
light, terracotta/autumn-gold/olive/cream color grade, natural texture, diverse
community representation, and no logos, readable text, or watermarks.
