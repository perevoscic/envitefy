The current evidence does not support release readiness. Multiple journeys reach publish successfully while losing the chosen format, title, time, or required guest instructions. These findings come from agent-simulated users, not customer interviews. Per-case claims and artifacts are in `.qa/create-campaign/2026-09-18/index.html`; the exact attempted and remaining matrix is recorded there.

Those milestones do not establish prompt or visual quality. ## Retest in this order

1. Reproduce each defect deterministically using the captured requests and drafts. Exercise the real extraction, generation-input, calendar and category-action code; avoid tests that merely assert source strings.
2. Retest the high-risk live cases after fixes: football, gymnastics, anniversary, housewarming, workshop, special-event lanterns, school open house, graduation and field trip. Verify both initial output and one appearance edit, plus save/resume, publication and guest actions.
3. Retest the affected category/output crosses after fixes. The 93-case matrix has no untouched combinations. Preserve the same ledger and identifiers: the completed Workshop, Game Day, Track/Field, Birthday and Wrestling recovery attempts retain their earlier infrastructure, budget or quality failures and associated costs.
4. Extend coverage with user-uploaded reference fidelity, redesign with an existing event, explicit Save/Discard/Keep editing, downloadable-file quality, signup-form capability requests, accessibility and keyboard interaction, varied timezones/DST and multi-day schedules, broader multilingual/locale cases, browser/network-interruption recovery, and controlled real email/calendar integrations. The bilingual briefs, observed repair attempts and deterministic boundary tests in this campaign do not cover all of these paths.
5. Run observed sessions with actual moms, teachers, coaches, gymnasts and general users. Ask them to complete their own task and explain surprises; synthetic persona scores cannot establish customer satisfaction.

## Evidence and reporting controls

Keep accepted artwork separate from rejected candidates and recovery output. A recovered original does not count as a successful redesign. Preserve the exact quality-check issues and repair instructions so a rejected improvement can be diagnosed. Distinguish assistant claims, client status text, canonical saved facts and published behavior.

Recorded costs come from the campaign gateway and may contain conservative usage bounds; they are not an invoice or a measure of Codex subscription usage. Calendar/Maps handoffs in this run are captured URLs, not completed navigation at external providers. Email remains in the isolated local sink. Infrastructure failures and corrected checker false negatives must not be presented as product defects.
