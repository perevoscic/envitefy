Welcome to the 38thAnnual Gasparilla Classic. 
 
Location:  Tampa Convention Center – 333 S Franklin St, Tampa, FL  33602 
 
Parking:  Parking is managed by the City of Tampa Parking Department.  Please keep in mind, parking rates 
change when there are events at Benchmark Arena or heavy event activity downtown. These rates for the lots & 
garages are determined by Parking and rates differ based on activity.   
 
Location of Lots and Garages: 
https://www.arcgis.com/apps/dashboards/12288a4774a9436b91953388b6770ff7 
Rates: https://www.tampa.gov/parking/info/parking-hourly-and-daily-rates 
Pay by Mobile App: https://www.tampa.gov/parking/programs/pay-by-phone-parking 
There is also a machine located at the garages to pay. Attendees must pay before leaving the parking garage. 
 
Please note that Disney on Ice has shows at Benchmark International Arena causing additional traffic and 
changes in parking rates.  Make sure to plan accordingly.

Ride Share:  There is a rideshare/Uber/Lyft/taxi drop-off spot just beyond the entrance to Front Drive at 333 S. 
Franklin St., across from Water Street.  This is the best location to drop-off persons with disabilities.  
 
Water Bottles:  We recommend sending your gymnasts with a water bottle.  There are bottle filling stations 
throughout the facility. 
 
Food/Beverage:  Outside food and beverages are not allowed in the building at Tampa Convention Center 
unless you have dietary restrictions/food allergies.  A variety of food and beverage outlets, including healthy 
items and Starbucks and Kahwa coffee brands will be available at the onsite concessions. 
   
Doors Open:  7:00am each day.    Please plan to arrive one hour before if your session is the first of the day.  
Plan to arrive 45 minutes before for all other sessions.   
 
Door Fees: Adults $25 per day, Children (5-17yrs) $20 per day  Door fees are daily.  There are no weekend 
passes. 
 
Event Merchandise:  A large selection of event merchandise will be availble.  Additionally, you can find a 
large selection of leotards, gymnastics apparel and accessories.   
 
Rotation Sheets:  There will be no hard copies of rotation sheets at the competition.  We suggest 
printing/downloading a copy from our website.  Make sure to refresh your browser to get the most updated 
copy.

Additional Info: 
• If you have any questions about the competition, please ask your coach prior to arriving in the Orlando 
area.  Your coach (and only your coach) can then email us if they cannot answer your questions. 
 
• Host Hotel Information- Make sure you book your hotels now.   
 
• Please allow extra time to arrive for your competition, especially on Friday when traffic can be heavy.  
Plan to allow 30-45 minutes to walk to the hall, register your gymnast and get to the competitive floor. 
 
• Official results will be posted on our website www.lightningcity.com.  Results will be posted the day of 
competition.  Live scoring will be available on meetscoresonline.com (internet connection permitting). 
 
• Daylight Savings Time begins on Sunday, March 8.  
 
• Please keep an eye on your children who are not competing for their safety.  We also ask you to leave all 
footballs, baseballs or all other objects for throwing at home for the safety of all the gymnasts competing 
and spectators in the building. 
 
• Only service dogs with a certificate are allowed at the Tampa Convention Center. 
 
The Tampa Bay Sports Commission has created a site for the event with a Visitor’s Guide, Maps and Deals 
and Discounts.

The Women’s Gasparilla competition will take place in the East, Central and West Halls of the Tampa 
Convention Center.  The registration area will be on the 2nd Floor near the Guest Services booth.  Entrance into 
the competition area will be by the West and Central Hall (near the Coffee Bar).  Admission tickets will be 
available for purchase right by the entrance to the competition area.
