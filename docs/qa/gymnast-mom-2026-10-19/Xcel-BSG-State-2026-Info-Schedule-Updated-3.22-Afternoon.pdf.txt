ATTENTION COACHES! 
 
Gym Rosters will be posted online. 
• Check your team rosters for the correct spelling of your athletes’ names and their session assignments!  
• Email corrections ASAP to info@usacompetitions.com  
• Make sure athlete birth dates are correct in Meet Reservations! Should an athlete compete in a session other than the one 
designated by her birthday, they will be ineligible for awards and the scores may not contribute to the team total.  
• Xcel Bronze and Silver will compete youngest to oldest. Xcel Gold competes by draw.  
 
USA Gymnastics Membership 
• Coaches: If you did not register attendance via Meet Reservations, you will not be listed on the sanction’s official sign-in sheet. 
In that case, have proof of professional membership available for verification.  
• All coaches will be required to sign the USA Gymnastics Coaches Sign In sheet.  
• Competing athletes and USA Pro members only allowed on the competition floor! 
 
Coaches Attire: At State Meets and above, the following dress code will be in place (USAG dress code rules) 
• Shoes—Closed toe athletic shoes 
• Shorts—Athletic or tailored shorts with a 7” inseam. Athletic or Docker style pants acceptable. No jeans. 
• Shirts—Collared or business casual with a gym logo. NO spaghetti straps, low cut tops or midriff revealing shirts  
• Hats—No hats or visors are allowed to be worn 
• Coaches who violate rules will be requested to leave the competition floor  
 
Awards Ceremonies: Individual and All Around awards will take place immediately following the end of each session.  
• Each session represents six different awards groups. 
• Award groups are divided equally by birth dates. 50% + 1 awarded for Individual and 100% All Around.  
 
Team Awards:  Take place following the final session for each level. 
• Team Divisions will be announced on Monday, March 30, 2026 
• Top five teams in each division will be awarded banners 
 
Spectator Admissions: Adults (13+): $26.00, Children (5-12): $16.00. Under 5 free. Cash Price is $1.00 less.  
 
Coaches hospitality: Lunch and dinner provided to coaches with back-to-back sessions during meal times. Please bring a re-
usable bottle for water. No outside food or beverage can be brought into the venue.  
If there is anything that we can do to assist you, please do not hesitate to contact us.  We wish you and your athletes the 
very best Championship experience!

COACHES INFORMATION—IMPORTANT! 
Coaches Sign In: Please sign the official USA Gymnastics Coaches Sign-In sheet, located at 
the check-in table in the lobby. If your name is not pre-printed on the sheet, sign the blank 
sheet and present proof of membership for verification (QR code). 
 
Master Rotation Sheet: Each floor music table will have a “Master” Rotation Sheet taped to it 
prior to each session. Please highlight your TEAM NAME at the top of the rotation sheet 
to indicate you are here. 
 
Scratches: If you have scratches, please use a PEN to CROSS OUT the athlete’s name 
on the Master Rotation Sheet. If you have any event scratches, please communicate that to 
the judges. If you have any questions, please ask the Floor Manager before doing anything. 
 
Format: After General Stretch all athletes will have a formal march-in on the floor exercise.  
Following introductions, Flight A will begin warming up their first event.  Once Flight A starts 
competition, Flight B can begin warming up on their first event. 
 
Rotations: We will be announcing all rotations.  Please remain at your event until you hear 
the rotation announcement. 
 
Hospitality: Coffee and tea provided. Bring refillable bottle for water. No outside food or bev-
erage can be brought into the hall. 
 
Floor Music: Please turn in your floor music during general stretch.  Make sure to retrieve 
your device!
