# Two new Envitefy shirt concepts

Created November 5, 2026, following the user's request for two more eye-catching designs.

1. **The group chat made it out.** Washed black cotton, acid-lime headline and electric-violet orbit. Small official e/airplane and wordmark front. Back wordmark, five social icons and YOU ARE INVITED.
2. **You had to be there.** Ice-blue cotton, cream/cherry lettering and coral sunburst. Same small branded front and back discovery footer.

Final concept mockups, each 1536 × 1024:

- `output/merch/new-concepts-2026-11-05/envitefy-group-chat-made-it-out.webp`
- `output/merch/new-concepts-2026-11-05/envitefy-you-had-to-be-there.webp`

Both were created with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool. Complete exact prompts and source paths are in `output/merch/new-concepts-2026-11-05/prompts-and-provenance.json`; image hashes and codec/decode checks are in the adjacent `verification.json`. These are garment mockups; no transparent production separations or print sheet were requested in this turn.

A marketing-focused AI reviewer proposed and visually reviewed the directions; no human consultation was performed. Root and independent review confirmed correct slogans, envitefy spelling, small front logo, full garment framing, Instagram/Facebook/YouTube/TikTok/Reddit in order and YOU ARE INVITED. Black/lime is the preferred attention-getting option based on visual contrast, not measured campaign performance.

Brand source inserts were the current official wordmark and e/airplane symbol, plus the existing five-icon reference board. Canonical app assets were left untouched. Exact original assets should be used when preparing production separations.

Both generated PNG originals were converted directly with FFmpeg libwebp quality 85, compression level 6. Actual WebP codec, unchanged dimensions and full decode passed. The exact originals were removed after validation. Display-only PNG derivatives were reconstructed from the final WebPs at the original gallery paths and retained under the existing display-cache exception to prevent the previously reported broken galleries. The final WebPs are the authoritative saved designs; original and derivative hashes are recorded separately.
