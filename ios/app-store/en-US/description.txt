Bring your event together with Envitefy.

Create invitations and event pages for celebrations, school activities, sports and community gatherings. Start with a design, upload an invitation or flyer, or describe your event with Envitefy Create.

Keep event information in one place
Share dates, locations, schedules and details through an event link. Collect RSVPs or build sign-up forms for volunteer roles, items and time slots.

Make it yours
Choose artwork and templates, adjust wording, and preview your event before sharing it. Save a draft when you want to return later.

Plan across devices
Use your existing Envitefy account and access the same saved events on the website and iPhone. Open directions, use calendar links and share event links with guests.

Envitefy requires an internet connection. Available tools depend on the event type and your account.
